## Supergeek Mode
Howdy folks, Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream just had its own Nintendo Direct this morning. I, being the casual and not obsessive by any means Mii fan woke up at the perfectly normal time of "Didn't go to bed" o'clock and watched the recording of the Direct as I woke up as any normal person did. And of course by recording I mean live as Youtube was recording it.
Anyway these new Mii variants have an unprecedented level of customization, being that now you can quite literally Microsoft Paint the faces to your liking. Those who hated the Miitopia makeup system are bawling on the floor.

So anyway I saw the thumbnail for the Direct recording come across my feed many hours later with all of the characters staring at the camera with some of them having a neutral expression and staring as Miis do. Then I started, as I do, noticing the feature layouts. Then I started thinking about how to recreate them.
Aaaaannnnnnndddddd you can see exactly where I'm going and it was all downhill from there.

So some rules, I'm using the Switch Mii Maker just because my Switch 2 was what was nearby. I don't really use the 99 color expansions even on Switch, like at all. I use the canon colors 💪.
I got as close as I could, a lot of new customization is here to backport. The eyes got a whole system to themselves practically. The "facial features", wrinkles and the like, also seem to be restyled a lot. This is most noticeable with Carlo. And obviously, like all new Miis, we got lots more hairstyles going on. Surprisingly this really only impacted a couple of Miis heavily. Jess got hit the worst. Also, ears. Breh.
This was a fun little experiment. Mocha was the one that surprised me. Like yeah I got nowhere close and that'll be the gut reaction, but seriously you go try and make Mocha. I was walking into it expecting to make an amalgamation for memes, was surprised how much of it I was able to mimic.
And yeah a lot of positioning could be probably be even more fine tuned. I just did this quick and dirty on a whim. This also means I just kinda hop scotched the first minutes of the Direct, I wasn't digging for every single Mii they showed - I know there was an alien in there somewhere briefly.

First up is Yoshiaki Koizumi, the Nintendo executive presenting it for the first minute or two and an interjection here and there. This one probably came out the most accurately. I imagine he had a preexisting Mii as Nintendo execs do that just got rendered in the TL3 engine and maybe some touchups to show off some of the new features.
The same person definitely made most if not all of these, that eye type and Angie's mouth type are going to be reused a lot. Or at least ones similar.

Meet Mocha, Angie's teddy bear. Yep, accurate representation of a Tomodachi Life save. Mocha is our, for this set of Miis, prime facepaint ~~over~~user. I thought I'd just be memeing by even trying, and to be clear I still was, but I was actually kinda pleased with being able to get kinda close at all. The nose, hair, and the eyes here are the only things I can be certain are actually Mii features, and I'm pretty certain the mouth is. everything else is, to the best of my knowledge, facepaint. The eyebrows on my recreation are putting in a lot of work. I did find an option to just have dots for eyes, which would technically be closer, but it looked a lot more lifeless without the 'sparkle' in the eye, so I opted for one with something similar.
